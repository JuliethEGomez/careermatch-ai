Our mission

Make career decisions with more clarity.

Job searching can be overwhelming. Candidates often apply without understanding which of their skills are most relevant to a role. CareerMatch aims to make that comparison simpler, clearer, and more actionable.

Try the Calculator

How CareerMatch works

1. Share your skills. Enter the capabilities and experience you want to highlight.

2. Add a job description. Paste the requirements for a role that interests you.

3. Review your results. Explore keyword overlap and identify areas to investigate or develop.

Who is it for?

CareerMatch is designed for students, career changers, and professionals who want a quick first look at how their experience relates to an opportunity. It can help people prioritize which job descriptions to review more closely and prepare thoughtful questions for networking conversations.

What comes next?

Future versions could add resume parsing, richer skill synonyms, tailored development recommendations, and links to professional networking resources. This student prototype intentionally uses a simple, transparent keyword comparison rather than making hiring predictions.
